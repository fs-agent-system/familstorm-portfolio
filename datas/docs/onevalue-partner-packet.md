## 3. Executive Quotation & Engagement Models
The capability document outlines 4 engagement frameworks for Japanese enterprise partners:

1. **AI-Driven Dedicated Team (AI-ODC):**
   - High-throughput dedicated pod delivering continuous feature development.
   - Sizing: 2–3 human senior leads (Architect, Lead Dev) orchestrating autonomous agent swarms, equivalent to 8–10 conventional developers.
   - Efficiency: 50%–60% lower Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) compared to standard Vietnam/India offshore rates.

2. **Turnkey System Development:**
   - Fixed-scope or milestone-based delivery for enterprise web applications, APIs, and gaming modules.
   - Delivery velocity: 2.5x to 3x faster turnaround (standard module cycle 24–48h; end-to-end multi-tier platform in 6–8 weeks).

3. **Technical Partnership / Joint Venture:**
   - Co-developing proprietary AI-native software products targeting Japanese or Southeast Asian markets.

4. **Strategic Investment & M&A:**
   - Open to strategic capital partnership, equity investment, or acquisition by Japanese technology groups looking to absorb proven AI-driven software operations.
